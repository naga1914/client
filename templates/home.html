Our Founders & Directors

Naga Ravuri

Mr. Naga has over 30 years of experience in delivering EPC and Project Development of Power, Renewable Solar and Wind farms and Oil and Gas Infrastructure construction projects across globe. He has worked with global companies like Saipem, Wood, and KBR, delivering multi-million USD EPC LSTK projects in energy sector on time and within budget. His expertise includes project execution, contract management, risk management, procurement, and strategic sourcing. BSR Prasad

Mr. BSR Prasad is a seasoned technologist and business leader with over two decades of experience driving innovation at the intersection of engineering and emerging technologies.
An M.Tech graduate from IIT Kharagpur and a B.Tech (ECE) graduate from JNTU Kakinada, Mr. Prasad began his career at DRDO, where he contributed to the development of mission-critical systems for national defense. He subsequently held senior leadership positions at leading global technology organizations, including HCL Technologies, HPE, iGate, and Infosys, successfully leading large-scale programs in Embedded Systems and Real-Time Technologies.
As the Founder, Mr. Prasad leverages his extensive expertise in embedded systems, real-time technologies, and AI/ML adoption to create innovative solutions that address complex real-world challenges. His leadership approach combines deep-tech research and development excellence with enterprise-scale execution, enabling the successful delivery of transformative technology solutions.
Under his guidance, the organization is focused on delivering intelligent and future-ready technology solutions for the Infrastructure and Building Construction industries, driving digital transformation, operational efficiency, and sustainable growth.
